# Privacy Policy — PairMind

Last updated: April 30, 2026

PairMind ("the app") is developed by Ben Sabakey.

## Data Collection

PairMind uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect the following data:
- Device identifiers (advertising ID / IDFA)
- Advertising data (ads viewed and interactions)

This data is used to display relevant ads and measure their performance.

## Third-Party Services

The app uses Google AdMob. Google's privacy policy is available at:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Data Storage

Game progress (score, settings) is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to external servers.
